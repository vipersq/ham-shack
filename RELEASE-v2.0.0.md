## Ham Shack 2.0 – maps, awards, live FT8, satellites & more 🎉

The biggest update so far. Your station website now shows **where you have been heard and worked**, **what you are hearing right now** and **how close you are to the next award**.

### Highlights
- 🗺️ **QSO map** – everyone in your log on a world map, with great-circle paths, grid squares and a day/night overlay
- 🏆 **Stats & awards** – DXCC, WAC, WAZ, ITU, WAS, VUCC and grid progress (worked / confirmed) plus charts
- 📡 **Live page** – FT8/FT4 decodes from WSJT-X, greyline, PSK Reporter and DX cluster
- 🛰️ **Satellites** – pass predictions and live positions for ISS, SO-50, RS-44, AO-7 and friends
- 💌 **eQSL & OQRS** – visitors download a personal QSL card and request paper cards online
- ⛰️ **Activations** – POTA / SOTA / WWFF map with automatic QSO counts
- 🎓 **Learn** – Koch Morse trainer, callsign copy and a Foundation-style quiz
- 🖼️ Gallery · ✉️ newsletter · 🔍 SEO · 💾 backup & export · 🇵🇱 Polish translation

### Rig Link 2.0
Now also listens to **WSJT-X**: live decodes on your site and QSOs logged in WSJT-X go straight into the logbook.

### Upgrading
1. Download `hamshack-theme.zip` below → **Appearance → Themes → Add New → Upload** → **Replace current with uploaded**.
2. Open any admin page – new pages, the **Radio** menu and data back-fill happen automatically.
3. Optional: **Ham Shack → Station settings** → set your exact latitude/longitude.
4. Using Rig Link? Replace it with `HamShack-RigLink.zip` and point WSJT-X UDP to `127.0.0.1:2237`.

Full list of changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/vipersq/ham-shack/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)

**Assets:** `hamshack-theme.zip` (WordPress theme) · `HamShack-RigLink.zip` (Windows helper)

Requirements: WordPress 6.2+, PHP 7.4+. Rig Link: Windows 10/11 with HRD and/or WSJT-X.
