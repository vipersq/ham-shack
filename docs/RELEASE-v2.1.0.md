## Ham Shack 2.1 – propagation, contests, notifications & an installable app

**Upgrade from 2.0:** download `hamshack-theme.zip`, go to **Appearance → Themes → Add New → Upload Theme**, choose **Replace current with uploaded**, then open any admin page. New pages are added to the **Radio** menu automatically. Rig Link stays at 2.0.

### 📡 Propagation
- **Live MUF map** from ionosondes (KC2G / GIRO) and *open / marginal / closed* for every HF band near you
- **Best times from my QTH** – a continent × hour heat map built from your own log
- **Space weather** (NOAA Kp) with an aurora alert, and **weather at the shack** with thunderstorm warnings

### 🏆 Contests
- Contests for the next 8 days imported daily from the **WA7BNM Contest Calendar** – they stay out of your calendar until you tick *I'm taking part*
- **Live scoreboard** on the home page while you are in a contest: QSOs, rate, DXCC, zones, last QSOs

### 🔔 Community & integrations
- Notifications to **Discord, Telegram** or any web hook (Zapier → Facebook): on air, new post, new DXCC, spotted, aurora, storm, sked
- **Club Log**: NEW ONE / new band / Most Wanted marks in the DX cluster and optional real-time upload
- **NEW ONE** badges in the logbook, a **QSL card wall** with flip-over cards
- **Book a sked** form with one-click accept (calendar event + e-mail with Google Calendar / .ics)
- **Repeater map**, **web SDR** player and **APRS** position on the Live page

### 📱 App & speed
- Installable **PWA** with offline mode, **light / dark** switch, WebP uploads, deferred scripts

Everything is configured in **Ham Shack → Integrations**. Full list in CHANGELOG.md in the repository.

**Attachments:** `hamshack-theme.zip` (the theme) · `HamShack-RigLink.zip` (Windows helper, unchanged)

73!
